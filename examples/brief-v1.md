# Synthetic Harbor Studio — brief v1

This is a fictional example, not a client engagement or accepted agreement.

The operations team wants a weekly draft of answers to five common customer questions.
Use only the product facts and approved answers supplied for that week.
Each answer must identify its supporting source and leave an explicit question when a needed fact is missing.
Deliver an editable answer draft and a short reviewer checklist.
The reviewer will decide whether an answer is accurate enough to send.
The workflow must not send messages, change accounts or claim customer acceptance.
The request currently covers English drafts only.
No delivery date, approved price, named reviewer or actual product-fact source has been supplied.
This brief authorizes preparation of a proposed scope only; it does not authorize implementation or sending.
