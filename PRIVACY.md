# Privacy and data handling — Viridis Scope Desk

This notice covers the skills-only Scope Desk source maintained by Viridis North LLC. It does not describe the host provider's service, another Viridis plugin or the separately hosted fleet MCP service.

## Inputs and outputs

You select briefs, source excerpts, baseline revisions and change requests in your chosen host. These may include personal or confidential information. Supply only information you are authorized to process in that environment and omit unnecessary identifiers.

The package provides instructions and reference text. It has no Viridis-hosted endpoint, credential collection, analytics, executable helper, remote connector or automatic transmission to Viridis. It does not independently store a copy of your conversation or files. It does not read a project merely because a URL or filename is mentioned; accessible source content must be supplied or made available through separately authorized host capabilities.

Draft scopes, change comparisons and evidence quotations can reproduce supplied information. They are data-bearing outputs, not anonymous summaries. Their accuracy and disclosure suitability require review. The plugin does not send a handoff, sign an agreement, authorize a price or publish a result.

## Host processing and retention

Your chosen host processes prompts, selected source content and generated responses under that host's applicable terms, settings and controls. Outputs may remain in its conversation or file history. This package cannot determine or promise the host's retention period, access controls or deletion outcome. Manage host-held content through that host's supported controls and manage any downloaded or copied handoff at its destination.

There is no independent Viridis account, uploaded-file service, storage-retention schedule or deletion service in this package. The absence of a Viridis endpoint is not a promise that host-held files disappear.

## Public support

When the source repository's public Issues route is available, use only a minimal high-level description and synthetic examples. Do not post client briefs, personal information, confidential source records, credentials or exploitable security details. Public support is not a confidential reporting channel. No private support route, response time, separate support-retention period or deletion guarantee is promised here.

A future hosted capability, integration or different data flow requires a separate accurate disclosure and authorized release. This notice does not authorize that change.
